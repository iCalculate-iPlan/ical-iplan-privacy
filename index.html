Privacy Policy

i-Calculate i-Plan My-Finance (package com.akn.i_financecalculator)
Last updated: 17 August 2026

The short version: everything you calculate or save — loan amounts, investments, saved scenarios, your optional profile, and your PIN — stays on your phone and is never uploaded to us or anyone else. What we do send to our servers is only what is needed to deliver notifications and in-app messages: your mobile number (used once to create your secure ID, then discarded — we never store it), that secure ID, a device identifier and notification token, your device/OS details, and which app screens you open. We never sell your data, we show no ads, and we use no advertising or analytics trackers.

1. Who we are

This app is published by Ishee Niraj ("we", "us"). If you have any question about this policy or your data, contact us at admin.icalculate@gmail.com.

2. Information that stays only on your device

The following is stored locally on your phone (in the app's private storage) and is never transmitted off your device by us:

  • Your name and 4-digit PIN used to unlock the app.
  • All values you type into calculators (loan amounts, incomes, rates, tenures, etc.).
  • Scenarios you save to My Vault, and the results shown in My Money Picture.
  • Any optional profile details you enter (age, annual income, metro/non-metro) used only to pre-fill calculators.
  • Reminders you add to the Money Calendar and your Fun Zone game scores.

Because this data lives only on your device, uninstalling the app permanently deletes it.

3. Information sent to our servers

To deliver push notifications and in-app messages, the app registers your device with our messaging service (api.inapptool.tech) and sends:

DataWhy
Your mobile numberSent once, only if you choose to enter it, so we can create your secure ID. It is used in that moment and discarded — we never store it, and it is never shared with anyone. You can skip this step.
Your secure IDA 10-digit code created from your mobile number, which cannot be turned back into that number. It is what lets your settings and notification history follow you if you reinstall the app or move to a new phone.
A device identifier and a Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) tokenTo send notifications and app messages to your device.
Device model and brand, Android version, app version, language, whether notifications are switched on, and how the app was installedTo show messages that fit your device and app version, and to know whether we are able to reach you at all.
Basic app activity — which app screens you open, app launches and unlocks, and whether you opened a notificationTo decide which message to show on which screen, and to measure whether our messages are useful. This never includes the figures you type into calculators.

This information is used only to operate notifications and in-app messages. It is not sold, not shared with third parties for their own use, and not used for advertising. We do not receive or store any of the financial figures you enter.

4. Third-party services

  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging — delivers push notifications. Google processes the FCM token per its privacy terms.
  • Frankfurter.app — used by the Currency Converter to fetch live exchange rates. Only currency codes (e.g. "USD", "INR") are sent; no personal data.
  • Our joke service — if you tap "Smarter (online)" in the Fun Zone, only the single word you type is sent to our server to generate a joke. No personal or financial data is sent, and the word is not stored.

5. What we do NOT do

  • No advertising, and no advertising or analytics tracking SDKs.
  • We do not sell or rent your data to anyone.
  • We do not access your contacts, photos, precise location, call logs, or SMS.
  • Your calculator inputs and saved scenarios are never transmitted to us.

7. Data retention and deletion

  • On your device: uninstalling the app deletes all locally stored data immediately.
  • Your mobile number: not retained at all. It is used to create your secure ID and discarded within the same request — there is no copy of it on our servers to delete.
  • On our servers: device/notification records age out automatically. To have your device record deleted sooner, email us at the address in section 1 and we will remove it.

8. Children's privacy

This app is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Security

Data on your device is stored in the app's private storage. Data sent to our servers travels over encrypted HTTPS connections.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, notify you in the app.

11. Contact

For any question, or to request deletion of the data we hold about you (see section 7), contact us at admin.icalculate@gmail.com. We will action deletion requests within 30 days.
