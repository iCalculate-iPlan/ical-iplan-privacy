Privacy Policy

App: i-Calculate i-Plan My-Finance · Last updated: 3 June 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the i-Calculate i-Plan My-Finance application ("the App") handles information. The App is a financial calculator that performs its calculations entirely on your device. Your privacy is important, and this policy explains, in plain terms, what data the App does and does not handle.

Information We Collect

The App does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal information. We do not require you to create an account, and we do not gather names, email addresses, contacts, location data, or financial account details.

Any values you enter into the App (such as amounts, interest rates, or time periods used for calculations) are processed only on your device to produce a result. They are not stored on any server and are not transmitted to us or to any third party.

PIN / Local Security

The App may use a PIN to lock access. This PIN is stored only on your device and is never uploaded, transmitted, or accessible to the developer or any third party.

How Information Is Used

Because the App does not collect personal data, there is no personal information to use, process, or disclose. All inputs serve only to display calculation results to you on your device.

Data Sharing and Third Parties

The App does not share any data with third parties. It does not include advertising networks, analytics services, or tracking technologies.

Permissions

The App requests only the permissions necessary for its core calculator functionality. It does not access your contacts, camera, microphone, location, or storage for the purpose of collecting personal data.

Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any personal information from children. Since no personal data is collected from any user, the App is suitable for general audiences.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date. Continued use of the App after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
